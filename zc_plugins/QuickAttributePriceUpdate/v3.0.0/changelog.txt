Quick Attribute Price Update -- changelog

v3.0.0 (2026-10-24)
  First release, numbered 3.0.0 to match its companion, Quick Price
  Update. For Zen Cart 1.5.8a through 3.0.0 on PHP 7.4 through 8.5.
  No core file is changed and no table is added.

  - Catalog > Quick Attribute Price Update: find attribute rows across
    products by product ID or ID ranges (12, 15, 40-55), by category with
    or without its subcategories, by option name and option value, by
    product name or model, and by product status; "Priced Rows Only"
    hides rows with no price; sort products and page the results.
  - Rows are grouped under each product, with its base price, its
    "Starting at" price, and links to the product, the Attributes
    Controller and (when installed) Quick Price Update.
  - Change the prefix (+, - or blank), the price and the one-time price
    in the table and save every changed row with one button. Values are
    checked before anything is written; a row someone else changed after
    the page loaded is skipped and reported, never overwritten.
  - Once per product touched, saving stamps the product's last-modified
    date and refreshes the storefront's price-sort value, so price
    sorting and "Starting at" prices stay right.
  - Notes flag what the row's price doesn't show by itself: free, display
    only, default, not discounted, not in base price, price factors,
    quantity prices, word or letter pricing, and a minus price on a
    priced-by-attributes product. Products whose own price is 0 but which
    aren't priced by attributes are flagged too.
  - A link in Catalog > Attributes Controller's action menu opens the
    product (or category) on screen here.
